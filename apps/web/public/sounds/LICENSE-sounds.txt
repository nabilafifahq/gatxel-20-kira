Kira sound effects

Source: Kenney (https://www.kenney.nl), audio packs "Interface Sounds", "Digital Audio"

Each file below is a short clip taken from a Kenney pack. The clips were trimmed at the
start, peak-normalised to about -3 dB, and re-encoded as mono 64 kbps MP3 so they are small
and play on every browser and on the Android WebView.
