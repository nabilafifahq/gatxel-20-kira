Kira sounds and music
=====================

All files here are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication,
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required; it is given anyway.
Every clip was trimmed, loudness-normalised and re-encoded as mono MP3 (56 kbps for effects,
24 kbps for music) so it is small and plays on every browser and in the Android WebView.
Each file is under 61 KB; the whole folder is about 0.4 MB.
