# Indonesian sales copy

**Tier: HOUSE.** Applies to every word an Indonesian client or user reads.

Everything in C-08 about hooks came from English-language A/B corpora. **Indonesian does not work the same way**, and this file is the part that is actually about Indonesian.

---

## R-00 The honest status of everything below

**There is essentially no experimental evidence for Indonesian copywriting.** We looked properly.

| Study | Design | N | Outcome measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nirmala 2020, *BIDAR* 10(2) | Descriptive corpus | 17 biscuit TV ads | **None.** Codes figures of speech |
| Rhamadany et al. 2023 | Qualitative, purposive | 63 data units | **None.** Frequency of ajakan, perintah, meyakinkan |
| Suharyo & Fitri 2011 | Simak dan catat | Newspaper classifieds | **None** |
| Susanto 2014, *IJAL* 4(1) | Observation, questionnaire, interview | Students | Politeness of `Anda`, and **not commercial** |
| Dwita 2018 | Interviews and FGD | 30 Jakarta professionals | Self-reported preference. Found English slogans favoured |

**Zero published A/B tests on Indonesian-language copy. Zero conversion studies. Zero controlled persuasion experiments.**

So two consequences, and both go in front of any client:

1. **Every rule below is documented convention with a named corpus behind it, not evidence.** The corpus is 16 live Indonesian sites read in August 2026: Mekari, Jurnal, Talenta, Qontak, Xendit, Midtrans, Accurate, Majoo, SIRCLO, BCA, Gojek, Tokopedia, Bibit, Ruangguru, Kredivo, DANA. Say "this is what the market does", never "research shows".
2. **Gatxel's own client A/B data would be more empirical evidence than the published Indonesian literature contains.** That is not a joke. Instrument it deliberately, and in two years we own the only real dataset on this.

## R-01 Register: the rule is zero pronoun first

**HOUSE.**

**The split is not B2B versus B2C. It is buyer sophistication.**

| `Anda` | `kamu` and `-mu` |
|---|---|
| Mekari, Jurnal, Talenta, Qontak, mid-market and enterprise SaaS | **Majoo**, which is also B2B but sells to warung and UMKM: "Saatnya majukan **bisnismu** bersama majoo" |
| Xendit: "mengintegrasikan **bisnis Anda**" | Bibit: "Bikin uang **kamu** bekerja lebih keras" |
| Midtrans: "Payment Gateway Indonesia Terlengkap untuk **Bisnis Anda**" | Ruangguru: "Diskon spesial **untukmu**" |
| BCA: "Senantiasa di sisi **Anda**" | Gojek: "membantu**mu** menjalani keseharian" |

**Correct a common assumption: `Anda` is not the premium register.** It is simultaneously the corporate register **and** the hard-sell direct-response and MLM register. Indonesian scam copy is saturated with it: "Jika tiba-tiba **Anda** meninggal dunia, bagaimana dengan keluarga tercinta **Anda**?" `Anda` on its own signals nothing. What it does is create **distance**. Whether that distance reads as respect or as a stranger selling you something depends entirely on what surrounds it.

### The actual rule: default to no pronoun at all

**Indonesian is discourse pro-drop. The addressee is recoverable, so naming them is marked, and naming them repeatedly is the loudest possible tell of English-first copy.** Most Indonesian hero headlines carry **no address term whatsoever**:

> Gojek: "Menghadirkan solusi untuk menghadapi tantangan sehari-hari"
> Mekari: "Platform software terintegrasi #1 di Indonesia"
> Xendit: "Infrastruktur pembayaran digital di Indonesia"
> Majoo: "Aplikasi Manajemen Bisnis, Operasional Serba Otomatis"
> Bibit: "Investasi Reksa Dana untuk Pemula"
> Kredivo: "Beli Sekarang, Bayar Nanti"

**Rule: default to zero. Introduce `Anda` or `kamu` only where the sentence needs a contrast or a possessive.**

### The possessive is also a layout decision

Indonesian lacks a compact possessive **in the formal register only**. `-mu` and `-nya` are clitics and compress hard:

| Formal | Casual | Saved |
|---|---|---|
| `bisnis Anda` (11) | `bisnismu` (8) | 3 |
| `untuk Anda` (10) | `untukmu` (7) | 3 |
| `memudahkan Anda` (15) | `memudahkanmu` (12) | 3 |

So choosing `kamu` shortens every possessive on the page. **This is the real reason Indonesian headlines run long, not word length.** Mekari has to write "solusi yang paling tepat untuk Anda"; Ruangguru writes "Diskon spesial untukmu".

### The rest of the address system

- **`Bapak/Ibu` does not belong on a landing page.** Zero of the 16 sites used it in page copy. It belongs in outbound letters, email openers ("Yth. Bapak/Ibu"), quotations and WhatsApp sales messages. On a web page it reads as a mail merge.
- **`-nya` as evasive third person** lets you reference the reader's situation without addressing them. Accurate does exactly this: "Kelola Bisnis Mudah, Accurate **Solusinya**!"

### The Gatxel split, as decided

| Audience | Register |
|---|---|
| B2B, consulting, government, finance, anything touching money or legal | Zero pronoun by default, **`Anda`** when one is needed |
| Consumer apps, UMKM and warung-facing tools, younger audiences | Zero pronoun by default, **`kamu` and `-mu`** when one is needed |

**Never mix within a flow.** Pick per project, write it into `DESIGN.md`, and never switch mid-funnel.

## R-02 The Indonesian headline, which is a different object

**HOUSE.**

**The dominant Indonesian hero headline is a noun phrase naming the category, plus a rank claim, plus "di Indonesia."** Six of sixteen sites. This is the "punchline or jargon" you have been seeing. **It is not a benefit headline and it is not a hook. It is a category-and-rank claim.**

> "Platform software terintegrasi **#1 di Indonesia**" (Mekari)
> "Software akuntansi dan operasional **#1 di Indonesia**" (Jurnal)
> "**#1 Software HR di Indonesia**" (Talenta)
> "Payment Gateway Indonesia **Terlengkap** untuk Bisnis Anda" (Midtrans)
> "Bimbel Online & Offline **Terbesar, Terlengkap, dan Terbukti** di Indonesia" (Ruangguru)

**Do not apply the English instinct to strip superlatives.** The `ter-` prefix is not a red flag in Indonesian the way "the best" is in English, and prefix repetition **is** the rhyme device. Ruangguru, a mainstream heavily funded brand, stacks three alliteratively.

**But C-08 still binds.** EPI 1.2.2 forbids `paling`, `nomor satu` and `ter-` **without documented proof on file**, and UU 8/1999 Pasal 9 forbids "kata-kata yang berlebihan". So the rank claim is available to us **only when we can evidence it**, and the evidence goes in the claims file. That is the difference between Mekari, which can prove it, and a slot site, which cannot.

### The structures that recur

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| Imperative with `-kan` or `-lah` | Kelola, Optimalkan, Tingkatkan, Dapatkan, Nikmati |
| Paired imperative, parallel rhythm | "Beli Sekarang, Bayar Nanti" (Kredivo) |
| `X, Y Solusinya` | "Kelola Bisnis Mudah, Accurate Solusinya!" |
| `Solusi X untuk Y` | "Menghadirkan solusi untuk menghadapi tantangan sehari-hari" |
| Assonance and rhyme | Xendit: "**Ringkas, tangkas**: Dukung pertumbuhan bisnis Anda" |
| Question opener | `Mau...?`, `Cara...`, `N Langkah...` |
| **The `#Hashtag` tagline** | `#PastiAdaJalan` (Gojek), `#MulaiAjaDulu` (Tokopedia), `#BukanDompetBiasa` (DANA), `#AwasModus` (BCA) |

**The hashtag tagline is a genuine Indonesian convention with no strong English equivalent.** Camel case, no spaces, and it functions as the campaign's memorable unit. The biggest brands all use it. Worth proposing to any client building a campaign.

**English loanword headlines are normal and high status.** Mekari, Qontak and Midtrans all mix untranslated English into Indonesian heroes. SIRCLO's hero is fully English. This does not violate C-06's one-language rule, which is about **interface** copy: buttons, errors and form labels must be one language. A marketing headline carrying an English technical term is native Indonesian practice.

**Length.** Practitioner guidance is 6 to 7 words. Our corpus averages 5 to 7 for noun-phrase heroes. **The compression lever is dropping the pronoun, not shortening words.**

## R-03 Affixation is the register dial

**HOUSE. This is the highest-value craft rule in the file, and it is convention, not evidence.**

| Form | Register | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bare root, `nge-` (`nulis`, `ngecek`) | Spoken, informal | Fine in `kamu` copy, wrong in `Anda` copy |
| `meN-` + `-kan` (`meningkatkan`, `mengintegrasikan`) | Neutral formal | The SaaS default |
| Imperative `-kan` (`Optimalkan`, `Tingkatkan`) | Direct | Standard CTA and section-heading form |
| **`peN-...-an` and `ke-...-an` nominalisation** (`peningkatan`, `pengelolaan`, `pemberdayaan`, `keberlanjutan`) | **Government press release** | **Avoid in marketing copy** |

The nominalisation chain is what makes copy read like a ministry. A real one:

> "**Peningkatan Sinergi** antar Kementerian **Mendorong Pertumbuhan** Ekonomi yang Berkelanjutan"

**Bureaucratic connectives that kill marketing copy on contact:** `dalam rangka`, `guna`, `adapun`, `sehubungan dengan`, `terkait hal tersebut`, and `di-` passives with no agent.

**Reduplication.** Attested persuasively but narrower than people assume: intensity and plurality (`buah-buahan`, `cepat-cepat`) and the fixed adverbials `sehari-hari`, `bersama-sama`. **Casual reduplication (`murah-murah`, `banyak-banyak`) drops the register sharply toward market-stall speech.** Use with care.

**Sentence-final particles.** Kulsum 2012 establishes that `deh` carries compulsion and `dong` carries coaxing, and that both are strictly non-formal. **Zero particles appeared in any hero or CTA in the corpus.**

| Particle | Where it is allowed |
|---|---|
| `yuk`, `nih`, `ya` | `kamu` consumer copy, WhatsApp and chat microcopy, social captions |
| `dong`, `sih`, `kok`, `lho` | **Nowhere on a landing page.** They presuppose a previous conversational turn, so they read as a pushy salesperson |
| Any particle in `Anda` copy | **Never.** It is a register break |

## R-04 What reads as scam or cheap in Indonesian

**HOUSE. This is the most valuable section in the file.** Indonesia has an enormous judi online, MLM and "bisnis online" copy register, and legitimate businesses borrow from it by accident.

### Burnt tokens: avoid regardless of context

Verbatim from live Indonesian gambling-SEO pages:

> "Situs Slot Online Gacor Hari Ini Gampang Maxwin Dengan Winrate Tertinggi **Anti Rungkad Boss**"
> "**388HERO :** Link Slot Garansi Kekalahan **Resmi** Agen Slot Maxwin Gacor **Anti Boncos**"
> "**WDBOS** Situs Slot Online Gacor **Resmi Terpercaya** Bonus Maxwin Setiap Hari"

**`TERPERCAYA` is the single most contaminated word in Indonesian commercial writing.** Also burnt: `RESMI` used as a boast, `GACOR`, `MAXWIN`, `ANTI RUNGKAD`, `ANTI BONCOS`, `GAMPANG MENANG`, `JP`, `WD`, `RTP TINGGI`, `BOCORAN`, `POLA`, `BONUS NEW MEMBER`, `DEPO`, `100% AMAN`, `DIJAMIN`, `PASTI`, `TANPA RIBET`.

### Structural markers

| Marker | Why |
|---|---|
| **Brand name with digits** (`CRS99`, `SLOT88`, `WDBOS`) | Never number a brand |
| **`BRAND:` or `BRAND |` prefixing the title tag** | Pure SEO-spam signature |
| **Emoji inside a `<title>` or H1** | Fine in a social caption, fatal in a page title |
| **Adjective stacking with no connective** | "Gacor Terbaru Resmi Terpercaya Paling Gampang". **The legitimate version is three maximum, comma separated, with `dan`**, as Ruangguru does |
| **`Hari Ini` or `Terbaru` appended to a headline** | Recency stuffing for SEO |
| ALL-CAPS mid-sentence, multiple `!!!`, red-on-yellow urgency | |
| **Raw `08xx` number in display copy** | Scam-coded. **A labelled WhatsApp button is not**, see R-05 |
| **Chat-screenshot testimonials** | Burnt. There is a live Indonesian market for fabricating them, including a "Fake Chat Testimoni Generator" and TikTok tutorials. Indonesian readers know. See O-02 |

### Investment and MLM markers

> "keuntungan besar dalam waktu singkat **tanpa risiko**", "**imbal hasil 10% per bulan**", "**kesempatan terbatas**", "tanpa modal", "passive income"

**Guarantee phrasing:** `Garansi uang kembali 100%`, `Dijamin 100%`, `Uang kembali tanpa syarat` are MLM and infoproduct coded. **The legitimate move is a specific, bounded, mechanical statement on the button itself:** "Coba Gratis 14 Hari" (Majoo), not a guarantee claim in prose.

## R-05 How to spot copy that was written in English first

**HOUSE. This is the AI detector for Indonesian, and it is the highest-leverage checklist here, because a model translates rather than writes.**

| Tell | What native copy does |
|---|---|
| **1. A pronoun on every clause.** "Anda dapat mengelola... Anda juga bisa... sehingga Anda dapat..." | Drops it. English needs an overt subject; Indonesian does not |
| **2. `Anda` or `-mu` on every noun**, calquing English *your*: "**bisnis Anda**, **tim Anda**, dan **data Anda**" | Names the possessor once, or not at all |
| **3. English title case on Indonesian function words** | Indonesian title case keeps `di, ke, dari, dan, untuk, yang, pada, dengan` **lowercase**. `Solusi Terbaik **Untuk** Bisnis Anda`, that capital U is a **reliable, greppable giveaway** |
| **4. `yang` chains** from English relative clauses: "solusi yang dirancang yang memungkinkan yang..." | One `yang`, or restructure |
| **5. Calqued idiom**: "Mulai perjalanan Anda", "Bawa bisnis Anda ke tingkat berikutnya", "Kami hadir untuk Anda" | Says the concrete thing |
| **6. `Kami` fronting.** "Kami menyediakan..." from *We provide...* | Prefers the imperative or the benefit frame. `kami` is for mission copy, not sales copy |
| **7. Generic dead phrases**: "Solusi Terbaik untuk Anda", "Kualitas Terjamin", "Pilihan Cerdas", "Untuk Semua Kalangan" | |
| **8. Wrong `Rp` or date formatting** | A native writer gets these right by habit. See R-07 |

**Greppable checks for CI:**

```bash
# English title case on Indonesian function words
rg '\b(Untuk|Dan|Di|Ke|Dari|Yang|Pada|Dengan)\b [A-Z]' --glob '*.{tsx,ts,md,json}'

# Pronoun stacking
rg -c 'Anda' content/ | awk -F: '$2>8 {print "R-05: Anda used "$2" times in "$1}'

# Calqued idiom
rg -i 'mulai perjalanan|tingkat berikutnya|kami hadir untuk|solusi terbaik untuk' 
```

## R-06 Trust language, and what Indonesian sites actually publish

**HOUSE.** Near-universal in the corpus:

1. **Full legal entity, spelled out, in the footer.** `PT Mid Solusi Nusantara` (Qontak), `PT Majoo Teknologi Indonesia`, `PT Cipta Piranti Sejahtera` (Accurate), `PT Espay Debit Indonesia Koe` (DANA). **The entity name usually differs from the brand, and Indonesian readers cross-check it against AHU and OSS registries, so it must be exact.** `PT` capitalised, no period. `PT.` is dated. **`CV` reads as materially smaller than `PT`.**
2. **Full street address with kelurahan, kecamatan, kota and postcode.** The administrative granularity **is** the signal. A bare "Jakarta, Indonesia" reads as offshore.
3. **Regulator names as logos and prose:** `Diawasi OJK`, `terdaftar dan diawasi Otoritas Jasa Keuangan`, `Bank Indonesia`, Komdigi PSE. Bibit prints its licence number, `KEP-14/PM.21/2017`.
4. **Certification marks:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022, PCI DSS, partner tiers.
5. **Counted social proof with `Dipercaya`.** This is the fixed Indonesian formula:

> "**Telah dipercaya oleh** 55.000+ bisnis Indonesia" (Mekari)
> "**Dipercaya oleh** lebih dari 35.000 bisnis di Asia Tenggara" (Jurnal)
> "**Dipercaya** bisnis terkemuka di Indonesia **sejak 2012**" (Midtrans)

`Dipercaya` is a passive with no agent, and it is the one bureaucratic passive that is idiomatic here.

6. **A `Legalitas` section** listing Akta Pendirian, SK Kemenkumham, NIB and NPWP is standard on SME-facing, agency and services sites, and in RFP responses. **For an Indonesian B2B buyer this is the page that gets checked.**

## R-07 CTAs, numbers, dates

**HOUSE.**

### Primary button verbs, ranked by frequency in the corpus

| CTA | Reads as | Seen on |
|---|---|---|
| **`Coba Gratis`**, `Coba Sekarang` | Strongest, lowest friction, no commitment implied. **The safe default** | Talenta, Qontak, Xendit, Accurate, Majoo |
| **`WhatsApp sales`** | **Indonesia-specific. Reads as responsive, not cheap** | Mekari, Jurnal, Talenta, Qontak, all as the **first** button |
| `Daftar Sekarang` | Commits to account creation. Higher friction than `Coba` | Midtrans, Xendit |
| `Jadwalkan Demo`, `Jadwalkan Konsultasi` | Mid-friction B2B. Better than `Hubungi Kami` because it names the next step | Majoo, Accurate |
| `Hubungi Sales` | Enterprise, secondary | Xendit, Qontak |
| `Hubungi Kami` | **Passive and weak as a primary** | |
| `Pelajari lebih lanjut` | Informational secondary | Midtrans |
| `Selengkapnya` | **Weakest. "More" with no object.** Fine inline, wrong on a primary button | Gojek |

**`WhatsApp sales` as a hero primary is the biggest divergence from Western SaaS convention we found.** Four Mekari properties place it **left of** the trial button. **Do not "correct" this for an Indonesian client.**

`Beli` and `Pesan` are transactional commerce only. `Konsultasi gratis` is credible in services but is also heavily used by info-product sellers, so pair it with a named consultant or a scheduling step.

### Currency, per PUEBI

- **`Rp`, capital R, lowercase p, no period, no space: `Rp5.000`.** `Rp.` and `RP` are wrong.
- **Thousands separator is a period. Decimal separator is a comma.** The inverse of English. `Rp1.000.000` and `Rp5.000,00`.
- **`Rp5.000,-` is wrong.** Ivan Lanin calls it "bidah", because no language reference gives that form.
- `rb`, `jt`, `M` are ecommerce and app conventions, closed up and lowercase: `10rb`, `5jt`. **Not in formal B2B or legal copy.** `M` is ambiguous between miliar and juta, so spell it out where money matters. **English `B` for billion is a hard foreignism.**
- **Practitioner reality diverges from the rule.** Real copy writes `Rp 5 juta`. Pick one house rule and hold it: **inconsistency within a page is the actual tell.**

### Dates, time, phone

- **Date: day, month spelled out, year.** `17 Januari 2024`. With a city: `Bandung, 17 Januari 2024`. `01/17/2024` reads American. `Jan 17, 2024` reads translated. Ranges: `1 s.d. 15 Agustus 2026`.
- **Time: 24 hour, period separator, zone.** `09.00 sampai 17.00 WIB`. `9:00 AM` is a foreignism.
- **Phone: `+62` form with the leading zero dropped for anything public facing.** `+62 812 3456 7890`. `08xx` is fine in body copy but scam-coded in a headline.
- Decimals: `4,5 juta`. **A site mixing `1,000` and `1.000` is machine translated.**
